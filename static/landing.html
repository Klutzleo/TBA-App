Built for the way people actually play now

Everyone's in a different city. TBA keeps the table feeling like a table.

Real-time by default

Every roll, attack, and ability is broadcast to the whole party the instant it happens, over a live connection — with a persistent log you can scroll back through mid-session or weeks later.

The mechanics do the talking

Multi-die attacks, Edge, BAP, Bonds that chain into Combos, hidden-difficulty Stat Checks, environmental hazards, Objects to pick or smash, the death spiral of The Calling — each one narrated for you as it resolves.

Play from your pocket

Install it like an app, get a push notification when it's your roll, and mirror the whole session to your group's Discord so nobody misses a beat.

Install it

Keep the table in your pocket

TBA is a Progressive Web App — add it to your home screen or desktop and it runs in its own window, launches from an icon, and can push you a notification the moment it's your roll. No app store, no download size.

On iPhone/iPad: tap Share then Add to Home Screen. On Android/desktop Chrome: menu → Install app.
