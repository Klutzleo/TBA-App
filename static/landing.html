The core of the system

No flat numbers. Every roll is a duel.

Nothing in TBA has a fixed difficulty. When you act, your die meets an opposing die — a defender's guard, the Story Weaver's roll for the world, a locked door pushing back. Each side adds what it's bringing: your stat, your Edge, your weapon or armor, a Tether pulling you on, BAP if you earned it. Whoever comes out ahead wins by the gap - and in a fight, that gap is the damage.

Attacks throw several dice at once, and every one of them fights its own fresh roll of the defender's guard. A flurry can partly land. One lucky die can still bite.

Why it's called that

Play it bold. The dice notice.

BAP — Bad-Ass Power. Do something bold, clever, or straight-up cinematic in the moment, run through the fire for a stranger, take the hit meant for a friend, pull off the plan nobody saw coming. If the Story Weaver buys it, your BAP counts on that roll. It's not currency you hoard and spend — it's the game rewarding you for thinking sideways and making a memory. How much it's worth climbs with your level, from +1 early to +5 by level 10.

Getting started

Whether you're joining a table or running one.

Find a game

Grab a join code from a friend running a table, or browse the public games looking for players.

Build a character

A few minutes, no classes or races, be who you want. Your Story Weaver waves you in.

Jump in

Roll, fight, and shape the story live in your browser, desktop, or phone. The log keeps every beat.

Create a campaign

Name your world and you're the Story Weaver, the app sets up the channels, dice, and tools.

Bring your players

Share one join code, or list the game publicly. Approve characters as they come in.

Run it live

Send checks, fire hazards, drop in Objects, track initiative, the app does the math, you tell the story.
