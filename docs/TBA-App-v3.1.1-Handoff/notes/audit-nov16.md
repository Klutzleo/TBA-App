## Contradictions and stale lines (both versions unless noted)

1. **Combos without an attack contradict themselves (Core and Kids Core, line 432).** "A heal or a buff doesn't get anything out of your free BAP bonus. So a Combo heal gets your free BAP bonus..." This is left over from before heals rolled stat + Edge. Fix: "A buff or debuff is a flat number, so your free BAP bonus does nothing for it. In a Combo, a buff or debuff counts one step higher on the buff table (the +4 maximum still holds). A heal is a normal roll, so it gets your BAP like anything else, and the once-per-person rule still applies."
2. **The SW Guides and Quick References never mention the attack-less Combo rule.** The QR Combo box also leaves out the main reason to take a Combo: both partners add their BAP automatically.
3. **Multi-die weapons vs BAP.** Core line 618 and SW line 475 say "Flat bonuses apply to each die, just like Edge," but BAP (Core line 73) goes on one die. Add "(BAP is the exception: it goes on one die)."
4. **Vehicle Captain and Pilot.** The SW station list says "(buff attack OR defense)," but Core and SW both say "They aren't buffs." Change it to "add to the attack OR defense."
5. **Unmanned stations.** The SW tip says "Unmanned stations don't function," then two lines later says a ship with nobody on defense still rolls. Add "except defense" to the first tip.
6. **"If both hit the same stat, the newer replaces the older" in a Combo.** Both effects fire at the same moment, so "newer" has no meaning. Pick a rule: the stronger one stays, or the acceptor's.
7. **Called counter has three names (Adult).** The text uses "Called count," "Times Called," and "death counter" (Core line 596). Pick one. The Kids version already uses "Times Challenged" throughout.
8. **"Rack up deaths" (Adult SW, Called tips).** A Mark isn't a death. Change to "rack up Marks."
9. **"A healer lifts the odds to about 65%" (SW Hard difficulty).** This came from a sim before heals added stat + Edge. The latest party sim (buffer, healer and Combo together) showed 56 to 62% at levels 7 to 9. Re-sim it, or soften to "noticeably."
10. **Marked by Death "loses 1 max DP"** is listed as a limitation, but the rule says the Mark "becomes a new Tether." A Tether is a roll modifier; lost max DP isn't. Either drop the DP example or say some Marks are limitations, not Tethers.
11. **"Weapon dice range from 1d4 to 1d12"** (Core, Making an Attack). The table goes up to 2d10 and 3d8. Change to "1d4 up to multi-die options."
12. **Duplicate sections.** "Magic Weapons & Bonuses" (Combat) and "Upgraded Weapons & Armor" (Items) say the same thing. Keep one.
13. **Emptiness range (Adult SW).** It says "starts at -1" but drops "(up to -3)," which the Core and the Kids SW both include.
14. **BAP effect "for 1 round"** isn't defined the way the 2-round buff is. Say "until the start of their next turn."

Low priority, worth knowing:
- Villain archetypes list a BAP number, but enemies only use BAP in Combos. Fine as long as SWs know that's what it's for.
- Single-pilot maneuvers use PP; the multi-crew Pilot uses IP. It's labeled "suggested," so this is OK.
- Memory Echoes and Grief run +1 to +3 while Tethers run +1 to +4. It reads as intentional; if so, leave it.
